UTM Grid Console

WGS 84 · Transverse Mercator · Krüger series

Input

South and west are negative. Paste a lat, lon pair into either box and it splits itself.

Inverse projection runs the Krüger series backwards with a Newton solve on the footpoint latitude.

Find a place

Geocoding by Nominatim (OpenStreetMap). No key, no account — please keep it to a few lookups at a time.

Map click

Clicking recomputes the zone in place. The view, zoom and pan are never touched — use Frame zone on the map if you want to jump. Drag the crosshair for a live reading.

Keys: G grid · M measure · T theme · F frame zone · Enter compute

Pinned points

NameZoneEastingNorthing

Points are stored in this browser only, under utmconsole.points. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Batch conversion

A header row is required. Recognised names: lat/latitude/y, lon/lng/long/longitude/x, zone, hemi/hemisphere, easting/e, northing/n, name/id/label. Every other column is carried through untouched.
